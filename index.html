AboutWhere I come from

I came up through web exploitation — Hack The Box's web penetration testing path. Recon, chaining bugs together, and writing the report that turns a finding into demonstrated business impact.

I am moving that into AI security. Most people entering this field arrive from machine learning; I arrive from the other side — from systems security, where the question has always been what an attacker can actually reach and what happens once they get there. I am building the ML half deliberately, and I am currently working through Hack The Box's AI Red Teamer path.

A jailbreak with no consequence is an anecdote. A jailbreak that reaches a tool call holding real credentials is an incident. I work on the second one.
